# Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales, profit, quantity, discount, and shipping cost data to identify important business trends and performance patterns.

## Objectives

- Analyze total sales and profit
- Identify high-performing categories and markets
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Identify top-performing products
- Understand the impact of discounts on profit
- Analyze shipping costs across markets

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Excel Charts
- Power BI
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization

## Key KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity
- Average Order Value

## Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard includes:

1. Sales by Market
2. Profit by Category
3. Monthly Sales Trend
4. Top 10 Products by Sales
5. Profit vs Discount
6. Shipping Cost by Market

## Skills Demonstrated

Excel | Pivot Tables | Data Cleaning | Data Analysis | Data Visualization | Power BI | Business Analysis

## Project Outcome

The analysis helps identify profitable markets, categories and products while providing insights into sales trends and business performance.
